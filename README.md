# India Alcohol Industry Analysis

Interactive analytical dashboard examining the interplay between economic performance, environmental impact, and consumer behaviour across India's alcohol beverage industry from 2018–2030.

## Project Overview

This project explores the relationship between consumption patterns, revenue generation, emissions, and carbon intensity across major alcohol beverage categories in India.

Using Power BI and DAX, the dashboard was designed to investigate how economic contribution and environmental burden vary across beverage segments over time.

## Analytical Framework

### Page 1 — Industry Structure & Environmental Burden

Key questions addressed:

- Which beverage categories dominate industry consumption, revenue, and emissions?
- Which categories contribute disproportionately to emissions relative to their economic value?
- Which beverages generate the largest environmental burden compared to their revenue contribution?
- How do consumption patterns differ from revenue generation across beverage categories?

### Page 2 — Growth Dynamics & Sustainability Trends

Key questions addressed:

- Is revenue growth keeping pace with emission growth over time?
- How has carbon intensity evolved across years?
- What role do population and consumption trends play in shaping emissions?
- Which beverage categories demonstrate the strongest decoupling between revenue growth and emission growth?
- How do long-term growth trajectories differ across beverage categories?

## Key Insight

The analysis highlights that the largest contributors to industry revenue are not necessarily the largest contributors to environmental burden. Examining revenue, emissions, and carbon intensity together provides a more complete understanding of category-level performance than any single metric in isolation.
